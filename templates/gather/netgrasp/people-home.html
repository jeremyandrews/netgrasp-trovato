The caller sets `ng_people` to a list of `ng_person_presence` rows: flat, keyed by the columns of the `ng_people_presence` view. The two times are epoch twins the view computes, because ng_people carries none of its own. A person with no `last_arrived_at_epoch` is still home: the daemon sets the state and the time together, but a mirror row written before the daemon ever saw that person has a state and no time. The card says "home" and no "since" rather than inventing one. #}

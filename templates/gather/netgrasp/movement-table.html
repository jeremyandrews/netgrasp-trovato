{# Arrivals and departures, one row each. Shared by /people/movements and the overview, which differ only in which rows and in which order. The caller sets `ng_movements` to a list of `ng_person_movement` rows, keyed by the columns of the `ng_person_movements` view: the daemon's `person_arrived` and `person_departed` events with their `details` lifted into columns. Every column but `id`, `event_type` and the time can be null, and each null is a real case rather than a broken row: - `person_name`: an event recorded without a person in its details. The daemon always writes one; an older or hand-written row may not. - `person_item_id`: the same, and then there is no owner page to link. - `location`: always, on a departure. A departure has a way out ("via"), not a place. - `via`: an arrival or departure with no edge access point behind it, which is every one on a network without UniFi enrichment. - the device columns: the device row was deleted, and `ng_events` keeps the event with `device_id` set to null. #}
